### `xbrl_in_pdf`

- Pairing: 537 of the 807 instances have a results PDF of the same BSE code on the same day. 12 of these pairs are left out because the PDF's period disagrees with the instance's. 525 are kept: 136 where the PDF's period agrees and 389 where the rules cannot read a period in the PDF. 270 instances have no results PDF that day. 52 kept pairs are of formats with no map and have no facts to check.
- The check can run on few of the kept pairs: the rules found no statement page for the statements of 284 of the 473 pairs that have facts, and none for the instance's basis in 70 more. Only 119 pairs have a page for their basis. So 47,717 of 56,857 facts in the kept pairs are `not_checked` with the reason `no_statement_pages`. The live check takes its pages from the full classification, which asks Gemini Flash, and finds more pages than the rules do. These counts are the floor.
- Of the 9,140 facts that were checked, 4,874 pass and 4,266 fail (46.7% fail). The share is 41.1% for the income statement, 46.3% for the balance sheet and 62.2% for the cash flow. It is 39.9% where the PDF's period agrees and 57.3% where the rules read no period, so a part of the failures comes from a PDF of another period than the instance's.
- Units: no unit stands out. A failure share of 43.0% (per share), 45.8% (crore), 46.4% (lakh) and 49.8% (million) does not show a PDF that prints another unit than the instance declares. Looked for directly, 26 of the 1,716 failed monetary facts that have pages are on the pages in another unit (2.0% for crore, 2.1% for lakh, 0.5% for million), and chance alone puts some there. The open question on the printed unit has no support on these pairs. The assumption that the PDF prints the unit the instance declares holds here.
- The elements that fail most are the five discontinued-operations lines (65 files each, the same files), the regulatory-deferral movement (64), `OtherExpenses` (59), the share of profit of associates (54), `ExceptionalItemsBeforeTax` (51) and `TaxExpense` (49). The five discontinued-operations lines fail in the same 65 files, which fits a PDF that leaves the block out when it is nil, but this run does not measure that.
- The rounded candidate earns no place on this data. All 4,874 passes are on the exact candidate and none needed the rounded one. It costs nothing to keep, and removing it changes the check, so it would bump `XBRL_PDF_CHECK_VERSION`. Decision left to the reviewer.
- Sign blindness is real. The check compares absolute values. Among 511 passes on a negative XBRL value, 375 print a bracket or minus and 136 print the number plainly. Among 474 passes on a positive value that a sum takes off (cash-flow outflows), 238 are printed signed and 236 plain. Among the 3,889 other passes on a positive value, 130 are printed signed. A pass says the number is on the page, not that its sign agrees.
- "Anywhere on a statement page" is generous. A value one unit of its last place away from the true one still passes in 23.3% of tests in lakh, 16.1% in crore, 7.5% in million and 5.4% per share. 559 of the 4,874 passes are on a value of zero, which any page with a zero passes, and 767 more are on a monetary value under 100 in the page's unit. Read the pass count with this chance level beside it.
- What to do with this: the check is evidence that a value was found, with a chance level of 5% to 23% by unit. A stronger check would compare the sign and look in the statement's own row (its label), not anywhere on the page. A change like this is a new `XBRL_PDF_CHECK_VERSION` with its own `--pdfs` run.
