## 8. How to sign

Sign once. It covers every Contribution you make afterwards.

1. Add a line for yourself to [CONTRIBUTORS.md](CONTRIBUTORS.md) in your first
   pull request, in the format `Full Name <email> (@github-handle)`.
2. Include the following statement in the description of that pull request:

   > I have read the Maester Contributor Licence Agreement and I agree to it for
   > this and all my future contributions to this project.

3. Sign your commits off with `git commit -s`. The `Signed-off-by` line it adds
   certifies your commit against the [Developer Certificate of Origin
   1.1](https://developercertificate.org/), and leaves a per-commit record of
   that certification in the project history.

If you are contributing on behalf of a company and need a signed agreement
between entities, open an issue and it will be arranged before your first pull
request is merged.
